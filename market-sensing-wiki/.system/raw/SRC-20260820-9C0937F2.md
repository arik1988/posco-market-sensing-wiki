### 보관된 원문 요약

The White House proclamation of 1 June 2026 states that products can be treated as composed entirely of United States steel for the relevant tariff regime when at least 85% by weight of their steel content was melted and poured in the United States. The accompanying fact sheet states that qualifying capital equipment can receive a 10% duty rate when it contains at least 85% United States melted-and-poured steel or smelted-and-cast aluminium by weight. The proclamation applies the updated tariff regime from 8 June 2026 through 31 December 2027.

This source note preserves facts stated in the White House proclamation and fact sheet. Exact eligibility depends on product classification and the applicable annexes.
