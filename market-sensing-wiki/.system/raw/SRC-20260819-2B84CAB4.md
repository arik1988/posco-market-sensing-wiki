# GB/T 45203-2024 recycled black mass for lithium-ion batteries

Published: 2024-12-31. Effective: 2025-07-01. Status: current.

China's national standards portal identifies GB/T 45203-2024 as the standard for recycled black mass for lithium-ion batteries. The responsible and standardisation department is the China Nonferrous Metals Industry Association; the issuing authorities are SAMR and SAC.

Official source: https://openstd.samr.gov.cn/bzgk/std/newGbInfo?hcno=EACBA5E2FAAB3A045E9DA9DDDEA9F88B
