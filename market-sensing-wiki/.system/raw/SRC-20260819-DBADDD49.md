# CATL TENER Sodium commercial launch

Published: 2026-06-22.

CATL unveiled its TENER Sodium stationary-storage system and scheduled initial Chinese customer deliveries for September 2026, cumulative shipments of 1 GWh by year-end 2026, and international delivery from June 2027. CATL said it added 40 GWh of annual sodium-ion capacity at Fuding and planned 160 GWh at Jining. The system shares the physical footprint of its LFP system and can switch chemistry without redesigning the enclosure.
