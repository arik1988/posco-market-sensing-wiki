# POSCO HyREX raw-material route

Publication date not stated. Verified: 2026-08-19.

POSCO explains that HyREX makes direct-reduced iron in multi-stage fluidised-bed reactors and melts it in an electric smelting furnace. POSCO states that the route can directly use medium- and low-grade sintering fine ore instead of high-grade pellets, while targeting blast-furnace-quality hot metal. The demonstration plant remains under design and construction.
