# GM adds LFP production at Spring Hill

General Motors reported on 14 July 2025 that the Ultium Cells plant in Spring Hill, Tennessee, would add lithium iron phosphate battery-cell production alongside its existing nickel cobalt manganese aluminum pouch cells. Ultium Cells is a joint venture operated by GM and LG Energy Solution.

GM said work on the LFP cell lines would start soon and full commercial production was targeted for late 2027. The company described LFP as a lower-cost solution and said a multi-chemistry portfolio would support both full-size electric pickups and affordable electric vehicles such as the Chevrolet Bolt.

This is relevant as observable customer behavior rather than a forecast: a major non-Chinese automaker is committing an existing North American cell site to LFP while retaining nickel-rich alternatives for other use cases. It supports a product-mix warning but does not prove that NMC or lithium-hydroxide demand disappears.
