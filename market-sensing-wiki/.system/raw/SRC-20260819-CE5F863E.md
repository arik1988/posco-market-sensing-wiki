# OECD Steel Outlook 2025: hydrogen DRI raw-material bottleneck

Published: 2025-05-20.

The OECD reports that the DRI-EAF route used by most producers requires low-impurity DR-grade pellets with iron content above 67%. Only 3-4% of current seaborne iron-ore shipments are of this quality, so ore availability may constrain the transition to hydrogen DRI-EAF.

Official source: https://www.oecd.org/content/dam/oecd/en/publications/reports/2025/05/oecd-steel-outlook-2025_bf2b6109/28b61a5e-en.pdf
