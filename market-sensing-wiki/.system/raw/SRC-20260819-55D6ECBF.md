# 원문 보존: POSCO Holdings partners with Hancock

- Publisher: POSCO Holdings
- Published: 2024-12-16; event: 2024-12-09 서명
- URL: https://newsroom.posco.com/en/posco-holdings-partners-with-australian-mining-company-hancock-in-lithium-business-for-rechargeable-battery-materials/

## 확인한 원문 발췌

> “pursue a lithium business venture with an annual production capacity of 30,000 tons.”

> “secure an additional stable supply chain … free from regulations imposed by the Foreign Executive Order Committee (FEOC).”

원문은 양사가 입지를 공동 평가하고 투자액은 후속 단계에서 정한다고 했습니다. POSCO는 2024년 기준 염수 25kt·광석 43kt 생산능력을 확보했다고 발표했습니다.
