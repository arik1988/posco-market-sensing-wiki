### 보관된 원문 요약

POSCO Holdings announced on 9 June 2025 that it had opened the Australia Critical Minerals R&D Lab in Perth. The company said the lab would combine Australian resources with POSCO Group materials technology and serve as a hub for raw-material processing technology and critical-mineral acquisition. The stated work includes steel and battery-material applications and rare-earth refining technologies with local companies and research institutions.

This is company execution context. It confirms POSCO Holdings' technology and acquisition exposure but does not independently prove the economics of a new critical-mineral project.
