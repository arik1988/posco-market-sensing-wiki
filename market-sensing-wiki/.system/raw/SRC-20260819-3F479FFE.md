# IEA Global EV Outlook 2026 — EV batteries

The International Energy Agency published Global EV Outlook 2026 on 20 May 2026 under a CC BY 4.0 licence. The electric-vehicle battery chapter reports that global EV battery deployment reached 1.2 TWh in 2025, almost 30% higher than in 2024.

For chemistry mix, the IEA reports that LFP exceeded 55% of globally deployed EV batteries in 2025, compared with nearly 50% in 2024. In emerging and developing economies excluding China, LFP powered two-thirds of electric-car sales and its share doubled from 2023. The European Union remained above 10%, while the United States share almost halved from a low base because of trade and sourcing constraints.

The IEA also reports that LFP packs were more than 40% cheaper per kWh on average than NMC alternatives in 2025, while noting that some of this gap reflects the application mix. More than 50 GWh of manufacturing capacity in the United States was reallocated toward LFP in 2025. The report says the rapid rise of LFP has dampened the demand outlook of NMC material producers.

Counter-evidence preserved in the same source: the United States did not follow the global LFP share direction in 2025, supply of LFP cathode material remains highly concentrated in China, and total EV battery deployment continued to grow strongly.
