# IRS Notice 2026-15 announcement

- Publisher: Internal Revenue Service, United States
- Published: 2026-02-12
- URL: https://www.irs.gov/newsroom/treasury-irs-provide-guidance-for-certain-energy-tax-credits-regarding-material-assistance-provided-by-prohibited-foreign-entities-under-the-one-big-beautiful-bill
- Collected: 2026-08-19

The Treasury Department and IRS announced guidance for determining whether eligible components
receive material assistance from a prohibited foreign entity and therefore become ineligible for
section 45X and related energy credits. Notice 2026-15 describes a material-assistance cost ratio,
interim safe harbours and example calculations while future regulations and safe-harbour tables are
prepared.

Taxpayers may rely on the notice for section 45X eligible components sold in taxable years beginning
after 4 July 2025 until the forthcoming safe-harbour tables are published. The notice requested
comments within 45 days on definitions, anti-circumvention rules and other issues.

This archived note is a factual extraction from the official announcement. It does not determine
whether a particular supplier, owner, component or transaction is compliant.
