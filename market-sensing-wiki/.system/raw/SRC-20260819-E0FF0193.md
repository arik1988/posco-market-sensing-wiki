# European Commission Industrial Accelerator Act proposal

Published: 2026-03-04.

The European Commission published COM(2026)100, a proposal for a regulation establishing measures to accelerate industrial capacity and decarbonisation in strategic sectors. The package includes the proposal, annexes, impact assessment and factsheet. The measure is a legislative proposal rather than enacted law; its procurement and support conditions remain subject to negotiation.

Official source: https://single-market-economy.ec.europa.eu/publications/industrial-accelerator-act_en
