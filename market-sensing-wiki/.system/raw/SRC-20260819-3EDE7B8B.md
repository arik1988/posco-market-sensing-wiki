# US DOE Supply Chain Readiness Level Preliminary Analysis — Batteries Summary

The U.S. Department of Energy's Manufacturing and Energy Supply Chains office published the battery supply-chain readiness summary in November 2024. The document maps upstream raw materials, refined materials, engineered components, integration and deployment for representative LFP and NMC battery supply chains.

On the supply-chain map, the DOE identifies lithium carbonate and lithium hydroxide as distinct refined-material routes. The map states that carbonate is preferred for LFP and hydroxide is preferred for NMC. This is the technical bridge used in the market-sensing analysis: a shift in battery chemistry can change the mix of lithium compounds demanded even when total lithium demand continues to grow.

The document does not quantify the exact substitution ratio between carbonate and hydroxide, nor does it disclose POSCO Holdings' customer contracts or production flexibility. Therefore the Wiki treats the direction of the product-mix exposure as verified, while leaving the financial magnitude and timing to internal sales, contract and process data.

This stored note preserves the relevant supply-chain mapping from pages 14–15. Company-specific implications and warning levels are separate analytical judgments.
