# IEA Global EV Outlook 2025 — chemistry economics and counter-evidence

The International Energy Agency published Global EV Outlook 2025 on 14 May 2025 under a CC BY 4.0 licence. The battery chapter reports that energy-sector battery demand reached 1 TWh in 2024 and projects EV battery demand above 3 TWh in 2030 under the Stated Policies Scenario.

The report states that LFP packs were almost 30% cheaper per kWh than NMC in 2024 and that LFP made up nearly half of the global EV battery market. It also records an important counterpoint: NMC retained an energy-density advantage. The report quantified LFP packs as roughly one-fifth lower by mass and one-third lower by volume than NMC packs, leaving NMC better suited to some long-range and cold-climate applications.

The IEA reported LFP below 10% in the United States during 2024, but above 10% in the European Union after adoption increased by about 90% for a second consecutive year. Southeast Asia, Brazil and India each exceeded 50% LFP share. These regional differences show that a global shift does not imply identical timing in every customer market.

This stored source note preserves the report's facts and scenario framing. It is used as historical persistence and counter-evidence, not as a claim that all NMC or lithium-hydroxide demand disappears.
