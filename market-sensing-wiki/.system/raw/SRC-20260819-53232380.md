# IRS Instructions for Form 7207 — December 2025 revision

- Publisher: Internal Revenue Service, United States
- Published: 2026-01-07
- URL: https://www.irs.gov/instructions/i7207
- Collected: 2026-08-19

The December 2025 instructions for the Advanced Manufacturing Production Credit state that,
for tax years beginning after 4 July 2025, no section 45X credit is allowed when the taxpayer is
a specified foreign entity or a covered foreign-influenced entity. They also exclude eligible
components that receive material assistance from a prohibited foreign entity, subject to the
specified transition wording for products sold before 1 January 2027.

The instructions require supplier certification when direct or indirect material costs are included
in the production-cost calculation for electrode active materials or applicable critical minerals.
Lithium qualifies when converted to lithium carbonate or lithium hydroxide, or purified to at least
99.9% lithium by mass. The applicable-critical-mineral credit is calculated under section 45X and
requires facility-level records and the relevant Form 7207 attachments.

This archived note is a bounded factual extraction from the official instructions. Eligibility of
any POSCO entity or project is not established by this source and requires ownership, sourcing,
facility, sale and tax-structure review.
