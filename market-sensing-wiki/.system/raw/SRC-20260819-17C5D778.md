# CATL and HyperStrong sodium-ion storage agreement

Published: 2026-05-06. Agreement signed: 2026-04-27.

CATL and HyperStrong announced a three-year partnership covering 60 GWh of sodium-ion battery supply for energy storage. CATL described it as the largest sodium-ion battery supply agreement to date and said the batteries use the same form factor as lithium-ion products, lowering adaptation cost for station deployment.
