# China Announcement No. 14 of 2025 on recycled black mass imports

Generated: 2025-06-10. Effective: 2025-08-01.

China's Ministry of Ecology and Environment and five other authorities announced that recycled black mass for lithium-ion batteries meeting Annex 1 requirements is not classified as solid waste and may be imported freely. Imports must be packaged, separated by category and inspected under customs technical rules. Customs code 3824999996 is provided for declaration.
