# 미국 철강 Section 232 관세 50% 인상

## 원문 보존

- 원문: https://www.whitehouse.gov/presidential-actions/2025/06/adjusting-imports-of-aluminum-and-steel-into-the-united-states/
- 발표일: 2025-06-03, 효력: 2025-06-04 00:01 EDT

> As of 12:01 a.m. eastern daylight time on June 4, 2025, the tariffs … are modified to increase the respective tariff rates from an additional 25 percent ad valorem to an additional 50 percent ad valorem.
>
> The modifications … shall be effective with respect to goods entered for consumption … on or after 12:01 a.m. eastern daylight time on June 4, 2025.

The proclamation applies the additional duty to steel articles and derivative steel articles, with separate treatment for the United Kingdom. It directs CBP to enforce declaration of steel content and states that no drawback is available for the duties.

## 교차검증

White House fact sheet, 2025-06-03, repeats the 25%→50% increase and effective date: https://www.whitehouse.gov/fact-sheets/2025/06/fact-sheet-president-donald-j-trump-increases-section-232-tariffs-on-steel-and-aluminum/
Reuters reporting via Investing.com confirms the doubling was activated on June 4: https://www.investing.com/news/stock-market-news/factboxtrumps-tariffs-and-threatened-trade-actions-4080709

## 사실/해석

확인된 사실은 미국 관세율과 효력일이다. POSCO의 미국향 물량·품목별 적용 여부와 실제 가격 전가는 내부 계약 확인이 필요하다.
