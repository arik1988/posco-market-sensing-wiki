# EU methane regulation requirements for fossil-fuel imports

Verified: 2026-08-19.

The European Commission states that from 1 January 2027 importers must demonstrate equivalent monitoring, reporting and verification rules, or OGMP 2.0 Level 5 plus verification for oil and gas, for contracts signed or renewed after 4 August 2024. Methane-intensity reporting follows on 5 August 2028 and an intensity limit on 5 August 2030. For pre-existing contracts importers must undertake reasonable efforts. Compliance and penalties sit with national authorities.

Official source: https://energy.ec.europa.eu/topics/carbon-management-and-fossil-fuels/methane-emissions_en
