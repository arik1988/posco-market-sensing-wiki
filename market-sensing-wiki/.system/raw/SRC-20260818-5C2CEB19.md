# EU steel import measure effective 1 July 2026

The European Commission states that its new steel measure applies from 1 July 2026. It caps tariff-free steel imports at 18.3 million tonnes per year and applies a 50% duty to imports above quota. The measure covers 30 steel product categories, except products from EEA countries. The Commission attributes the measure to structural global steel overcapacity and trade diversion risk.
