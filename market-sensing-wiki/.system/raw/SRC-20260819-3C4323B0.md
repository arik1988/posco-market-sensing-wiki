# IRS final regulations for section 45X — calculation context

- Publisher: Internal Revenue Service, United States
- Published: 2024-12-16
- URL: https://www.irs.gov/irb/2024-51_IRB
- Collected: 2026-08-19

The final regulations state that the section 45X credit for an applicable critical mineral or
electrode active material equals 10% of the taxpayer's qualifying production costs. Lithium is an
applicable critical mineral when converted or purified into the forms and purity specified by the
rules. Extraction costs may be included only under the conditions described in the regulations,
including that the taxpayer claiming the credit incurred the qualifying United States or United
States-territory costs.
