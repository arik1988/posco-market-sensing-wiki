# European Steel and Metals Action Plan

## 원문 보존

- 원문(EUR-Lex COM(2025)125): https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/ALL/?uri=celex:52025DC0125
- 발표일: 2025-03-19

> These sectors all share major common challenges for their competitiveness: high energy costs, exposure to an unlevel playing field in international competition, decarbonisation investment needs and regulatory burden.
>
> Direct electrification remains the most efficient pathway for decarbonising several forms of steel and metal production, while others will depend on indirect electrification methods, such as hydrogen produced from electrolysis, increasing the share of energy costs in total production costs.

Action-plan measures include energy-cost relief, demand-side measures for low-carbon products, support for decarbonisation investments and trade-defence action. The Commission also states that a protective measure after 30 June 2026 is needed.
