# Australian domestic gas reservation announcement

On 7 May 2026, the Australian Government announced a domestic gas reservation scheme intended to begin on 1 July 2027. LNG exporters will be required to supply gas to the Australian market equivalent to 20% of exports. The government explicitly expects the measure to put downward pressure on domestic gas prices and states that detailed implementation consultation will continue before commencement.
