### 보관된 원문 요약

The UK Government states that its new steel trade measure took effect on 1 July 2026. Overall tariff-free quota volumes were reduced by 51% compared with the prior safeguard measure, and imports above quota face a 50% tariff. The measure applies to steel products that can be made in the UK. A transitional exemption applies to qualifying goods contracted before 14 March 2026 and imported between 1 July and 30 September 2026, subject to verifiable evidence.

This source note preserves facts stated on GOV.UK. Product-level quota availability and POSCO contract exposure require customs-line and customer data.
