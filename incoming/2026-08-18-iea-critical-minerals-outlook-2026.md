# IEA Global Critical Minerals Outlook 2026 - lithium signals

The IEA published the Global Critical Minerals Outlook 2026 on 16 July 2026. Its executive summary states that lithium prices more than doubled amid strong energy-storage demand and constrained supply after the 2023-2024 downturn. The IEA projects lithium supply deficits through 2035, although the gap has narrowed as more projects are expected. It also reports that lithium-company investment fell around 40% in 2025 and lithium exploration spending fell around 45%.
